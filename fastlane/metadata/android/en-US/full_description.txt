Lean is a VPN client for Android built on the sing-box core, with its own
interface, routing and subscription handling. It works with any server or
subscription; there is nothing to sign up for.

Protocols: VLESS/Reality, VMess, Trojan, Shadowsocks, Hysteria2, TUIC,
WireGuard and AmneziaWG with DPI-resistant obfuscation, plus Mieru, olcRTC and
VLESS over XHTTP through helper processes.

* import share links, subscriptions and .conf / .json / .yaml configs
* subscriptions can be filed into folders and collapsed away
* split tunnelling per application, kill switch, custom DNS, LAN bypass
* rule-based routing with geoip/geosite rule-sets
* per-node latency measurement and automatic selection of the fastest server
* quick settings tile, dark, light and AMOLED themes, live theming
* no ads, no accounts, no analytics
