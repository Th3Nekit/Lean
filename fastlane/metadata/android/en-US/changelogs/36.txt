"Speed" — a speed test inside the app: download, upload, latency and jitter.
While the VPN is on it can measure twice against one and the same server, through
the tunnel and around it, so the difference is what the tunnel actually costs.
Inside Russia the target comes from a built-in list, because Speedtest's own
directory is blocked there.

"What works right now" — a reachability check of the services themselves:
YouTube, Telegram, Discord, ChatGPT, Steam and the rest, one by one, with an
answer for each.

"Why it does not work" — DPI probes: what exactly breaks the connection (SNI,
outbound UDP, DNS forgery, teardown under load) and which settings follow from
that.

"Keep the system from sleeping the tunnel" — on by default. The most reported
fault was "the VPN switched itself off": in deep sleep the core could not answer
the far end in time and the session was closed. The tunnel now keeps ticking, at
the price of battery.

"Recover the connection by itself" — when a tunnel goes silent and then fails a
real request made through it, the app resets connections and DNS and reconnects.
Outages are written to a log.

The F-Droid build no longer contacts api.github.com on launch: F-Droid does its
own update checking, and the manual check button stays in both builds.

This build has no promotion of the author's paid service in it.
